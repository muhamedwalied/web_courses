## Bottom line for a downstream consumer

If you need to build anything over this content, this repo gives you **prose, not data**.
There is no schema, no IDs, no license, and no two circles agree on a heading vocabulary.
The only stable, machine-usable signal is the **file path**; the only imported-but-typed
structure is the raw HTML tables in `Data Science/README.md`, `BackEnd/Laravel/README.md`,
and `BackEnd/DotNet/README.md`. Any normalization would require hand-authored per-circle
adapters, and you would be parsing five or more distinct dialects (`Phase`/`Module`/`Week`,
`Level 0–4`/`Objectives`/`Plan`, `Beginner Level`/`Objectives`/`Resources`,
week-file-per-topic, and Notion-link-only stubs). Also budget for one CP1256/NEL-encoded
file and an uninitialized submodule whose content lives in a third-party repo last touched
in March 2024.
